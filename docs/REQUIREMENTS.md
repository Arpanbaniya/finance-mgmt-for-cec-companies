No unimplemented domain is marked complete. Ordinary manual journals now persist and consume independent frozen decisions through scoped submission/review/post screens and routes. K014 ordinary demo manual-journal acceptance is complete, including freshly approved controlled dated reversal. This is not completion of R1 or of any later financial-source module. Approval queues, broader financial adapters, typed event consumers, scheduled dispatch, worker/site scopes and full statutory behavior remain pending. Job inspection/manual recovery covers only policy activation alerts. Policy activation is not financial posting or statutory-rule activation. R1-APPROVAL/K010 and R1-REPLAY/K011 remain partial until broader source workflows and typed delivery/recovery are integrated and accepted. Journal events are queued, not claimed delivered. R1 is not complete; no later release is started.

K012 setup includes account masters, explicit fiscal calendars, complete purpose mappings and transaction-internal numbering. K013/K014 consume retained scoped account/mapping/master versions with the source number and enforce posted-journal archive dependencies. Open-item and opening-balance guards remain requirements before those modules ship. Closing/reopening/year-end transitions remain K042. No financial history, balance or statutory outcome is fabricated by setup.
